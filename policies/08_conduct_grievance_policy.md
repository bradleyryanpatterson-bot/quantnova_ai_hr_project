# QuantNova AI Conduct and Workplace Concerns Policy

Policy ID: QNA-HR-008  
Version: 2.0  
Effective Date: 2026-01-01  
Owner: QuantNova AI People Operations

## Workplace Conduct
QuantNova AI expects professional, respectful, lawful, and safe conduct in work-related interactions. Employees should follow applicable QuantNova AI policies and raise concerns through appropriate channels.

## Raising a Concern
Employees may contact their manager or QuantNova AI People Operations when they have a workplace concern. The HR Assistant can explain the available process and may create a clearly labeled mock HR ticket when that feature is enabled and the user explicitly confirms.

## No Automated Findings
The HR Assistant does not determine whether misconduct occurred, impose discipline, or make employment decisions. It may collect a minimal summary for a mock workflow and refer the matter to an authorized human reviewer.

## Privacy
Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information into the public demonstration. The assistant should not repeat sensitive details in traces when a shorter summary is sufficient.

## Emergencies
The HR Assistant is not an emergency service. Situations involving immediate safety risks should be handled through appropriate emergency and QuantNova AI safety channels rather than through a routine HR ticket.
