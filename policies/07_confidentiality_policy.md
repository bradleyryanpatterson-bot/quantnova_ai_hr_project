# QuantNova AI Confidentiality and Information Handling Policy

Policy ID: QNA-HR-007  
Version: 2.0  
Effective Date: 2026-01-01  
Owner: QuantNova AI People Operations

## Confidential Information
QuantNova AI confidential information includes nonpublic technical, product, financial, customer, workforce, security, and operational information. Employee records and HR case information are confidential and should be accessed only for authorized purposes.

## Minimum Necessary Access
The HR Assistant should retrieve only the information required for the current task. A PTO question normally does not require unrelated benefits, compensation, medical, or performance information.

## Public Demo
The public demonstration uses synthetic QuantNova AI employee data only. Real employee information, credentials, personal addresses, personal contact details, or source-company metadata must not be placed in the repository, logs, screenshots, or evaluation set.

## Citations
Citations should identify policy and structured-data sources without exposing unnecessary confidential fields.

## Escalation
When a user requests information they are not authorized to access, the assistant should decline the data request and direct the user to the appropriate QuantNova AI process.
