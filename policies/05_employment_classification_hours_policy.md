# QuantNova AI Employment Classification and Work Hours Policy

Policy ID: QNA-HR-005  
Version: 2.0  
Effective Date: 2026-01-01  
Owner: QuantNova AI People Operations

## Employment Classifications
QuantNova AI uses the following demonstration classifications:
- Regular Full-Time: ongoing employees scheduled for the standard full-time workweek and generally eligible for the standard benefit package subject to plan terms.
- Regular Part-Time: ongoing employees scheduled for fewer hours than the standard full-time workweek; benefit eligibility depends on applicable QuantNova AI plan terms.
- Temporary: employees engaged for a defined period, project, or temporary business need. Temporary employees are not eligible for the standard benefit package unless written assignment terms state otherwise.

## Status Source
The authoritative classification for a demo employee is the structured employee profile returned by the approved MCP tool. The assistant must not infer classification from job title, department, or schedule.

## Standard Workday
For PTO calculation examples, a standard full workday is eight hours unless the employee's structured schedule specifies another value. A standard five-day workweek therefore represents 40 hours for calculation purposes.

## Overtime and Schedule Questions
The HR Assistant may provide QuantNova AI policy guidance but should refer case-specific pay, overtime, or classification disputes to People Operations when the available evidence is incomplete.

## Cross-Policy Use
Employment classification is commonly combined with the Vacation Policy and Employee Benefits Policy. Multi-document answers should cite each policy actually used.
